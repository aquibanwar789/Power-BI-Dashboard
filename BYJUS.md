📊 BYJU’S Financial & Performance Dashboard (2017–2025)
📌 Project Overview

This project presents a Business Intelligence Dashboard analyzing the financial and operational performance of BYJU’S from 2017 to 2025.

The dashboard provides insights into:

📈 Revenue Growth

📉 Profit / Loss Trends

💰 Debt Analysis

👨‍🏫 Employee Count Over Years

🎓 Student Distribution (Rural vs Urban)

📊 Funding vs Advertisement Spending
